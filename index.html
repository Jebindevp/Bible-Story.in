David and Goliath – Victory Through Faith

“The battle is the Lord’s, and He will give all of you into our hands.” — 1 Samuel 17:47

When the giant Goliath defied the armies of the living God, young David stood fearless. Armed not with heavy armor or spear, but with unshakable faith and the name of the Lord of hosts, David stepped forward onto the battlefield.

With a single stone and unwavering trust, God delivered an extraordinary victory. This story teaches us that no giant in our life is greater than the power and faithfulness of our Almighty God.
